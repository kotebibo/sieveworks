# Sieveworks brand — "Daylight Arcade"

Owner-picked 2026-09-11 (direction 2 of 4, street-tested). The product is
serious verification wearing a friendly face: an average person should feel
at home, and the living demos (flock, fractal, swarm) carry the color while
the chrome stays calm.

## Tokens (source of truth: `app/globals.css`)

Light (default):
- bg `#F5F7FA` · panel `#FFFFFF` · well `#ECF0F6`
- text `#1E2430` · dim `#5A6478` · faint `#6E7889`
- **sky `#2F79CE`** — the interactive voice (links, primary buttons, active nav)
- **amber `#C77414`** — renders & energy only
- **green `#1E9E5C` / red `#D6452E`** — strictly verified / rejected
- shadows: soft `0 4px 18px rgba(30,36,48,.07)`; pop (primary buttons)
  `0 6px 20px rgba(47,121,206,.28)`

Dark = navy night variant (`.dark`), not the retired terminal black.

## Type

- Display: **Nunito** 700–900, tracking −0.015em — rounded, confident
- Body/UI: **Inter**
- Numbers: **Spline Sans Mono**, tabular — every number, always (unchanged law)

## Shape & motion

- Radius: 14px panels, 12px buttons/wells; soft shadows, no hard borders
- Terminal artifacts retired: corner ticks, background mesh (light), mono labels
- Motion laws unchanged: data-change flashes, one hero entrance, hover
  delight (sheen), everything behind prefers-reduced-motion

## Color semantics (the relaxed "one accent" rule)

Multi-hue is allowed ONLY by meaning: sky = interactive, amber = render,
green/red = verdicts, fitness ramps red→green. Never decorative rainbow.

## Voice

Plain, warm, honest. "Your browser can earn its keep." Never "earn passive
income with idle hardware" (zero-winner phrasing, assessment §1b).
